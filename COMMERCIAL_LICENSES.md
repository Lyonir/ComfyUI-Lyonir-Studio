## Qwen3-TTS
The official Qwen3-TTS code/model family is Apache-2.0.

## Brazilian checkpoint
The PT-BR route uses the QwenTTS checkpoints inside
`acidente/fala_pb_checkpoints`. The repository is a mixed-license collection;
its model card identifies the QwenTTS branch as a fine-tune of
`Qwen/Qwen3-TTS-12Hz-1.7B-Base` and lists the base license as Apache-2.0.

Users should still review the current upstream model/repository terms before
commercial deployment.

Open-source/model licenses do not grant permission to clone or commercially
exploit another person's voice. Consent, publicity/personality rights,
contracts and dataset/performer rights are separate.
